FAQs

What is Frontend Mentor, and how can it help me?

Frontend Mentor offers realistic coding challanges to help developers improve thier frontend coding skills with projects in HTML, CSS and Javascript. It's suitable for all levels and Ideas for portfolio building.

Is Frontend Mentor free?

The majority of challanges are free. Some are premium and require a Pro subscription. It will clearly say "Free" or "Premium" for each challange.

Can I use Frontend Mentor projects on my Portfolio?

Absolutely. You are free to use any projects you complete on the platform on your portfolio, whether for personal or commercial purpose (within their lisence term)

How can I get help if I'm stuck on a challenge?

The best way is to join the Frontend Mentor Discord community, where thousands of developers gather and there is dedicated "help" channel for questions
